Our Mission

Experience can become a lifeline.

Surviving Fentanyl is being built to help people and families affected by fentanyl addiction through honest education, peer-informed support, prevention, community outreach, and connections to treatment and recovery resources.

Education

Clear information about fentanyl, overdose risk, naloxone, prevention, and the realities of addiction.

Recovery

Encouragement, practical guidance, and connections to resources for people ready to begin or continue recovery.

Community

Outreach that brings people, families, organizations, and resources together around one goal: saving lives.

Why This Exists

From surviving to helping others survive.

Surviving Fentanyl was created from lived experience with fentanyl addiction and recovery. The goal is not to judge people struggling with addiction. It is to reach them, understand them, help them find resources, and show that another life is possible.
