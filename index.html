My Story

From surviving to helping others survive.

Surviving Fentanyl was created from lived experience with addiction, loss and recovery.

The goal isn't to judge people struggling with addiction. It's to reach them, understand them, connect them with real resources and remind them that another life is possible.

This organization is being built around a simple idea: experience that nearly destroyed a life can become experience that helps save one.

“If my story can help even one person choose a different tomorrow, it's worth telling.”
