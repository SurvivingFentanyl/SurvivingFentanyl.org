Suspected Opioid Overdose

Treat it like an emergency.

If someone cannot be awakened or is not breathing normally, call 911. Give naloxone if you have it and follow the 911 operator's instructions.

01
Call 911

Get emergency medical help immediately.

02
Give Naloxone

Use naloxone if available. More than one dose may be needed.

03
Support Breathing

Follow the 911 operator's instructions and provide rescue breathing if instructed and able.

04
Stay With Them

Keep watching their breathing and stay until emergency help arrives.

Naloxone / Narcan

Carry something that can save a life.

Naloxone is an opioid-overdose reversal medication. It can reverse overdoses caused by opioids including fentanyl.
